# Adventure-Work-Sales-Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed using the AdventureWorks dataset to analyze sales, orders, returns, customers, products, profitability, targets, and regional performance.

The project focuses on transforming business data into interactive visualizations and meaningful business insights.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze AdventureWorks business data and develop an interactive Power BI dashboard that helps understand overall sales performance and customer behavior.

The dashboard aims to:

- Monitor key sales and business performance indicators
- Analyze revenue, profit, orders, and returns
- Identify high-performing products and customers
- Understand customer purchasing patterns
- Compare actual performance with targets
- Analyze business performance across different regions
- Present complex business data through clear and interactive visualizations

---

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Calculations and KPI development
- **AdventureWorks Dataset** – Source dataset
- **Data Visualization** – Interactive charts, tables, cards, slicers, and maps

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Overview

The Sales Overview page provides a high-level view of overall business performance.

**Visuals include:**

- Revenue Trending
- Orders by Category
- Top 10 Products
- Monthly Orders
- Monthly Revenue
- Monthly Returns
- Most Ordered Product
- Most Returned Product


---

### 2. Customer Analysis

The Customer Analysis page focuses on customer performance and purchasing behavior.

**Visuals include:**

- Customer Trending
- Orders by Income Level
- Orders by Occupation
- Customer Details
- Top Customer
- Customer Orders
- Customer Revenue

The page also provides year-based filtering for analyzing customer performance across different years.

---

### 3. Target & Profit Analysis

This page focuses on business targets, profitability, and return trends.

**Visuals include:**

- Monthly Orders vs Target
- Monthly Revenue vs Target
- Monthly Profit vs Target
- Profit Trending
- Return Trending
- Product Performance


---

### 4. Regional Analysis

The Regional Analysis page provides a geographical view of business performance.

Users can filter the analysis by:

- Europe
- North America
- Pacific
- Select All

The map visualization provides a geographical perspective of sales activity across different regions.

## 💡 Business Insights

The dashboard helps identify several important business patterns, including:

- Revenue and profit trends over time
- Changes in order and return volumes
- Categories generating higher order volumes
- Products contributing significantly to orders and revenue
- Products with higher return rates
- Most ordered and most returned products
- Customer groups based on income level and occupation
- High-value customers based on revenue contribution
- Monthly performance compared with business targets
- Regional distribution of business activity

These insights can support better understanding of sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and regional trends.
